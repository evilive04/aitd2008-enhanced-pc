Generation briefs (descriptive record, not verbatim tool-call transcripts):

- Slate: seamless square neutral charcoal slate, fine fractured stone flakes,
  low contrast suitable behind pale serif labels; orthographic, evenly lit,
  no text, frame, roots, vignette or lighting hotspot.
- Roots: transparent square upper-left L of rough dark woody roots, substantial
  braided main bundle with natural fine tendrils and muted bronze highlights;
  long arms along the top and left, empty interior; no stone, metal frame,
  lettering, leaves or luminous effects. Real volume rather than smooth cables.

Integration preserves texture proportions. Slate uses tiled world-coordinate
UVs; root corners use a uniformly scaled square with rotation, not independent
horizontal/vertical stretching. Text, outlines, icons and keycaps are independent
vector geometry. The PNGs supply the authored surface and organic detail.

**Phone policy:** the phone and every phone subpage (GPS, messages, contacts,
dialer and settings) use no roots. The clean dark typography/frame language can
remain coordinated with the rest of the game.
